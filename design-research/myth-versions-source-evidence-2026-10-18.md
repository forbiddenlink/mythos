The comparison cells identify their passage bounds. An `absent` cell means the selected passage does not narrate that moment, not that its author denied it. No new cells are marked `lost`: no claim of textual loss is needed for these excerpts. Positive differences use `variant` and an explanatory note. Apollodorus's report of an alternative beauty-rivalry story is labelled as such, rather than merged into his main narrative. Ovid's scene is attributed to Perseus's report; the older English rendering does not justify a new claim about consent.

The narrow layout repeats each source's full set of moments in a labelled definition list. The desktop layout retains the table with column and row headers. Both layouts use the same underlying cells. A wrapping ordered chronology retains explicit source dates and numbering without positioning close or coincident date labels on top of one another. This replaces proportional time spacing; readers can compare dates directly, but no longer see interval lengths drawn to scale.
