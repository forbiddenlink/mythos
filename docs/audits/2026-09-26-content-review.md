# Content review, 2026-09-26

Scope: thirteen specific claims flagged for source-checking across the Greek, Finnish, Inca, Korean, Akan, Tlingit/Haida, Canaanite, Diné and Aboriginal Australian entries, plus cultural-sensitivity reads of the Diné and Aboriginal Australian records. Each check lists the claim, the source consulted, the verdict and the change made. Paraphrases keep the repo's "(paraphrase)" convention; no quotations or line numbers were invented, and a locator was only added where the source showed it.

Verdict key: **Verified** (source confirms, no change), **Corrected** (data changed to match the source), **Clarified** (data was defensible but imprecise), **Not verified** (source could not be reached; data left as is, or softened).
