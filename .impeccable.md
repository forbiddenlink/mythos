## Design Context

### Users

Mythology learners, students, and design-conscious engineers/recruiters. Primary job: move from orientation (pantheon/story) into deeper study (deities, trees, places) without feeling like a generic SaaS wiki. Portfolio audience needs spectacle that stays fast.

### Brand Personality

Classical · scholarly · luminous — dark-academia atlas of antiquity. Evoke wonder and trust, never neon “AI product” energy. Calm authority with occasional ritual sparkle (gold, candlelight, parchment).

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Direction:** Editorial classical atlas — Cinzel display, Crimson Pro body, Source Sans UI; OKLCH gold / midnight / parchment / bronze / patina.
- **Hero:** One cinematic composition (“The Atlas Opens”); brand name is the hero signal; ≤2 ambient motion systems; scroll-scrub GSAP, not idle GPU spam.
- **Surfaces:** Prefer manuscript lists, metadata strips, and borderless prose over card soup. Cards only for interactive or structured data clusters.
- **Motion:** Purposeful state/attention only; `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses cinematic to static; animate transform/opacity only.
- **Anti-patterns:** Purple/indigo SaaS gradients; Lucide-in-gradient-square feature grids; slate/amber stock heroes; stacked identical cards; glow-ring icon badges as the brand mark; flat single-color backgrounds without atmosphere.

### Design Principles

1. Brand first on promotional surfaces — Mythos Atlas must remain the dominant first-viewport signal.
2. Theme tokens (`bg-card`, `text-foreground`, gold family) over hardcoded midnight skins on content bodies.
3. Depth on few surfaces (home signature + deity template) beats shallow polish on every route.
4. Interactivity should feel like exploring a physical atlas (hover, scrub, reveal) — never delay reading.
5. Accessibility is non-negotiable: contrast-tuned gold text, skip link, reduced motion, keyboard paths.
