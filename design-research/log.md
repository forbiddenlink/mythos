## Final interaction and evidence mapping

- W1: refs 05, 07, 08 support keeping search, count and mode context visible. Ref 07 demonstrates a URL-addressed query; no external Back behavior is claimed. The proposed persistence change is grounded in the local reproductions, not inferred from gallery imagery.
- W2: refs 04 and 12 support clear browse/recovery choices; refs 10 and 14 support coherent option semantics and keyboard selection. The proposed styling uses Mythos’s own command-row conventions.
- Preservation: refs 01, 02, 03 and 13 support existing category entry points, typography hierarchy, attributed imagery and source-based reading. No new brand direction is warranted.
- Ref 11 did not produce zero results; it is retained only as a comparison for editable queries and recovery suggestions.
- Ref 14 keyboard test: focused the listbox and pressed End; selection moved to the final Oganesson option. Screenshot: shots/refs/14-wai-example-keyboard.png. This tests the example only, not Mythos or screen-reader compatibility.
