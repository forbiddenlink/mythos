# Primary-source excerpt verification ledger

Reviewed 2026-09-18. This is an evidence ledger for the 35 structured
`primarySourceExcerpts` in `deities.json` and `stories.json`, not a claim that
the remaining catalogue has been verified. “Verified” below means that the
displayed wording was compared against the identified public text or edition.
It does **not** establish a manuscript reading, a critical edition, or
permission to reproduce modern translations.

`Locator/text` means the work and passage are supported, while the displayed
wording has not been compared to the named translation or edition. `Adapted`
means the record deliberately says so in the data after this review.
`Unverified` means that the record remains useful as a lead but must not be
represented as a direct quotation. URLs are evidence leads and should be
rechecked before a future scholarly edition pass.

## Corrections made in the data

- Replaced the false Thor direct quotation with a labelled paraphrase: the
  proposal is Heimdall's and Thor objects in _Þrymskviða_ 14–16.
- Corrected the Hávamál locator to stanza 138.
- Replaced inaccurate generic “Kojiki, Chapter 17” locators with the Upper
  Volume and the Amaterasu/Susanoo episode.
- Corrected the Ramayana edition-numbering conflict for the `Rāmo
vigrahavān dharmaḥ` verse.
- Removed unsupported blended-translator claims and marked composites,
  paraphrases, and failed locators in existing visible fields.
- Marked Ra's English-only record so it no longer masquerades as a verified
  hieroglyphic transcription.

## Contract and rendering now implemented

Every one of the 35 records now carries `quoteStatus`, `verification`,
`sourceUrl`, and `edition`. `sourceUrl` is constrained to HTTP(S) by the data
schema. The source-excerpt component and source-detail pages show an explicit
status before the text. Editorial paraphrases and unverified entries no longer
use quotation marks or blockquotes; original-language switching is limited to a
direct quotation with a verified transcription. Do not add more displayed
quotations without all four fields and ledger-level evidence.

## Legacy deity-source-note inventory

`deities.json` has 201 older `primarySources` notes. They use only `text`,
`source`, and an optional `date`; they are not part of the 35 structured
excerpts and cannot meet the new direct-quotation contract. The deity page now
renders them as **Source Notes** with an explicit statement that their wording
has not been verified as direct quotation, rather than as blockquotes.

| Inventory result                                                                | Count | Treatment                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Legacy notes                                                                    |   201 | Keep as unverified source notes until individually migrated.                                                          |
| Source labels that name a translator/translation                                |    71 | Leads for a future edition-by-edition pass; a named translator is not sufficient evidence by itself.                  |
| Labels that explicitly say composite, retelling, or compilation                 |    10 | Retain only as editorial leads; they must not be migrated as direct quotation without an exact witness.               |
| Oral, chant, or tradition-labelled notes                                        |    40 | Require community-appropriate, edition/performer-aware sourcing; do not force them into a Western text-edition model. |
| Modern intermediary labels (for example Spence, Gregory, Budge, Anderson, Gray) |    38 | Distinguish the modern publication date/translator from the date of the underlying tradition.                         |

This pass did **not** verify all 201 legacy notes: that would require a
source-by-source edition project and, for oral traditions, appropriate
community and performance context. It did correct the demonstrable identity and
date errors found in the inventory:

- The old Ryūjin note presented a _Kojiki_ passage about Watatsumi as if it
  named Ryūjin. It now identifies the later association.
- The old Ebisu note presented Hiruko as if the _Kojiki_ directly named Ebisu.
  It now identifies the later association.
- The old Daikokuten note presented Ōkuninushi as if the _Kojiki_ directly
  named Daikokuten. It now identifies the later syncretism.
- The Osiris/Budge date no longer places Budge's 1895 translation in the New
  Kingdom.

The priority migration order is the 71 translator-labelled notes, then the 38
modern intermediary notes, before adding more source-note prose. Keep the
remaining notes visibly unquoted and do not promote them to structured excerpts
without an exact passage, a single edition/translator, a stable HTTP(S) source
URL, and a verification disposition.
