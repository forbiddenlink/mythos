This document catalogs all 413 entities currently rendered with procedural archival plates.
It is organized worst gap first (heroes, pantheons, showcase-visible deities, followed by remaining deities, creatures, stories, locations, and artifacts).
Each entity includes an image generation prompt formatted in the Mythos Atlas house style from scripts/generate_missing.py.

- P0 (Critical Showcase Gaps: 51 entities): 31 heroes, 14 pantheon covers, and 6 showcase-visible deities (Loki, Cronus, Baal, Gaia, Hecate, Helios).
- P1 (High Impact Content: 81 entities): 34 top-ranked major deities (importanceRank 1 to 3) and 47 mythological creatures.
- P2 (Depth Content: 281 entities): 130 remaining deities, 64 stories, 63 mythical locations, and 24 legendary artifacts.

## Do-not-generate policy

Sacred figures, places, stories and objects from living Indigenous traditions must never get AI-generated imagery. They keep their procedural archival plates permanently. This covers Aboriginal Australian, Diné (Navajo), Haudenosaunee, Inuit, Tlingit and Haida, Yoruba, Akan, and Polynesian/Maori entries (Yoruba and Akan religion and Maori sacred figures are living religious traditions).

- 130 rows are marked `DO NOT GENERATE` in the table below (16 P0, 63 P1, 51 P2). They stay in the table only so the plate count of 413 stays reconcilable.
- Generatable backlog: 413 minus 130 = 283 entities.
- Showcase surfaces (home hero, collections, pantheon covers, gods-of) prefer entities whose provenance is `illustration-ai` and otherwise fall back to the existing plate, so no AI image can be introduced for these entities.
